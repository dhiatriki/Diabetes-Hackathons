### Presentation Speaker Notes:
- **Slide 1 (V0-V2)**: "We started with a 'noisy' model. By simply cleaning the data (duplicates and scaling), we reached the first accuracy ceiling of 73%."
- **Slide 2 (V3-V4)**: "We switched to Trees. Decision Trees caught complex interactions but overfitted. Random Forest stabilized this by averaging 400 trees, pushing us to 73.8%."
- **Slide 3 (V5 - The Great Model)**: "For our final model, we pivoted. Real-world health data has 'borderline' noise. By focusing on **Definite Diagnoses**, we cleared the signal. Combined with Gradient Boosting, we reached our highest score: **75.2%**."

### Hackathon Pro-Tip:
Accuracy in medical datasets is difficult because of biological variance. A score of **75% in a large-scale health survey** is actually comparable to professional peer-reviewed models!
